Lost it? ReClaim it.

A decentralized Lost & Found — register your valuables on an immutable smart contract, finders report them with GPS, and you claim them back with a secret code. No middlemen. No lost paperwork. Just proof, on-chain.

🎯

Our Vision

A world where every lost item securely finds its way home. By anchoring ownership and recovery to the blockchain, we make losing things recoverable — permanently and trustlessly.

🚀

Our Mission

To streamline the recovery of lost physical assets using decentralized Web3 technology — one smart contract on BOT Chain that anyone, anywhere can use to tag, find, and reclaim what matters to them.
