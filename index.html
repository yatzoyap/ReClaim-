Vision

A world where every lost item finds its way home. Anchoring ownership and recovery on the blockchain makes losing things recoverable, permanently and without trust.

Mission

Streamline recovery of lost physical assets with one smart contract on BOT Chain that anyone can use to tag, find and reclaim what matters to them.
