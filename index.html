Vision & Mission

Welcome to ReClaim, built for the Girl Meets Tech Build Week Hackathon Vol 2. Our mission is to streamline the recovery of lost physical assets using decentralized web3 technologies. We envision a world where every lost item securely finds its way back to its owner through immutable smart contracts.
