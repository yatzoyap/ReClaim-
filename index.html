ReClaim

Tag your stuff and ReClaim it.

Register something you own and stick the QR tag on it. If it goes missing, whoever finds it can scan the tag and report where it is. The record lives on the blockchain, so there's no company in the middle.

A sample tag. Give your item a name and receive its own QR code.

  1. Register the item Name your item and receive a public code.
  2. Attach the tag Print the QR tag and stick it on the item.
  3. Someone finds it They scan the tag and report where they found it. Your item switches to Found.
  4. Reclaim it back Press Claim on the item. It goes back to Registered and the tag is ready to reuse.
